## What GetFit is

GetFit calculates an estimate of your body composition from measurements you
enter, builds a training program around it, and adjusts that program from the
workouts you log.

**GetFit is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.** It does
not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Consult a doctor before
starting a new training programme, particularly if you are pregnant, injured, or
have a heart, joint or metabolic condition. Stop exercising and seek medical
help if you feel chest pain, light-headedness or shortness of breath.

Your body-fat figure is an estimate produced by a published formula, not a
measurement of your body. With tape measurements GetFit uses the US Navy
circumference method, which typically lands within 3–4 percentage points of a
DEXA scan; without them it falls back to a height-and-weight estimate, which is
less accurate still and is labelled "Estimated" wherever it appears. Muscle mass
is derived from the body-fat figure rather than measured. Treat all of it as a
way to track change over time.

Training loads are a starting point calculated from what you have logged, not an
instruction. You are responsible for training within your own limits.
