## Common questions

### How is my body-fat estimate worked out?

From the tape measurements you enter — waist, neck, height, weight and, for
women, hips — using the published US Navy circumference formulas. It is an
estimate, not a clinical measurement, and it is not medical advice. Nothing is
guessed from your photos, and no AI service is involved.

### Do I have to add a photo?

No. Progress photos are entirely optional and the app works fully without one.
A photo you do add is private: it is never shown to anyone else, never analysed,
and never used for advertising or to train any model.

### Why can I only take one assessment a week?

Body composition does not move meaningfully day to day, and measuring more often
mostly measures noise — water, food and time of day. A new assessment unlocks
seven days after the last one.

### Symmetry shows "—" instead of a number

That means no limb measurements were entered for that assessment. GetFit shows a
dash rather than inventing a figure. Add left and right arm and thigh
measurements and the next assessment will carry a real score.

## Membership

### What does a membership include?

Personalised workouts, progressive overload, guided sessions, weekly body
analysis, and progress and goal tracking.

### How do I cancel?

Your subscription belongs to Apple, not to us, so it is cancelled in your
device settings:

**Settings → your name → Subscriptions → GetFit → Cancel Subscription**

You keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for. Deleting
your GetFit account does **not** cancel the subscription — cancel it first.

### I paid but the app still shows the paywall

Tap **Restore purchase** on the membership screen. That asks Apple directly what
you own. If it still does not unlock, email us with the date of purchase and we
will sort it out.

### Refunds

Apple handles all refunds for purchases made in the App Store. Request one at
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). We cannot issue
refunds ourselves.

## Your data

### How do I delete everything?

**Settings → Privacy & data → Delete account.** That removes your measurements,
assessments, photos and training history. It is immediate and cannot be undone.

### What do you collect, exactly?

See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html). The short version: your measurements and
training history, your email address, and any photo you chose to add. No
tracking, no advertising, and nothing sent to any AI service.

## Health and safety

GetFit is a fitness tool, not a medical device, and nothing it produces is
medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new training programme,
particularly if you have an injury, a heart condition, or are pregnant. Stop
exercising and seek medical help if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of
breath.
